## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The EU→IT premium used vehicle import market is large, structurally favorable, and competitively fragmented. The overall Italian used car market reached 5.59 million transfers in 2025 (+2.9% YoY), valued at approximately €84.5B. The premium segment (BMW/Mercedes/Audi) is the single largest driver of B2B cross-border trade from Germany — which supplies 77% of all vehicles purchased by Italian dealers on B2B platforms. No operator in this market currently combines proactive scouting, success-fee pricing, and dedicated B2B outreach in South Italy. That gap is ARGOS's position.

**Key findings:**
- Price delta DE→IT remains 15-30% for premium models, structurally stable
- BMW, Mercedes, Audi are the top three brands in Italian used premium demand
- All direct B2C competitors (Bolidem, Autotedesche, Importami) have grown review volume but have not pivoted toward dealers
- AUTO1 is scaling aggressively (725 branches, +32.5% YoY) but buys FROM dealers — not competition, potential channel
- AutoProff B2B auction average €8,910 (petrol) / €10,680 (diesel) — below ARGOS's premium target
- No new B2B scouting entrant has been identified in the South Italy territory

## 1. MARKET SIZE — EU→IT PREMIUM IMPORT

### 1.1 Italian Used Car Market (total)
- **2025 transfers**: 5,593,646 (+2.9% vs 2024) — source: ACI
- **2025 market value**: ~€84.5B (Mordor Intelligence estimate)
- **2026 forecast**: ~€89.3B (+5.6%)

- **February 2026 YTD**: +10% vs prior year — strong demand signal

### 1.2 Premium Segment
- BMW leads the Italian used premium ranking for the second consecutive year, followed by Mercedes and Audi
- These three brands are the most imported across all 17 European cross-border trade corridors studied by carVertical (2025)
- Premium used is structurally the entry point to brand ownership in Italy — domestic new-car prices are the highest in the EU for these brands

### 1.3 B2B Cross-Border Flows (AutoProff data, Q1 2025)
- **77% of vehicles purchased by Italian dealers** on B2B platforms originate in Germany
- Secondary source: Denmark (fleet returns, electric vehicles)
- Average transaction price: **€8,910 petrol** | **€10,680 diesel**
- Dominant dealer strategy: import petrol/diesel from Germany, export electric to Northern Europe
- Premium imports (BMW/Mercedes/Audi) represent the highest-value segment, typically €15,000-45,000 per vehicle — significantly above the AutoProff average

**Implication for ARGOS**: The €800-1,200 fee on €20,000-40,000 vehicles represents a 2-4% cost of goods — structurally sound and below the Importami benchmark (4% + IVA). ARGOS is priced correctly for the premium B2B segment.

## 2. PRICE DELTA DE→IT

### 2.1 Current Spread (Q1 2026)
- **Headline range**: 15-30% savings vs Italian market price (all sources consistent)
- **Used cars**: savings can reach 40% in favorable cases
- **Concrete example from Bolidem**: BMW X6 2020 ~100k km → €5,000+ cheaper in Germany vs Italy equivalent
- **IVA mechanics**: vehicles >6 months and >6,000 km = used for VAT purposes — IVA paid once in Italy at immatricolazione, no double taxation

### 2.2 Trend Assessment
The 15-30% spread has been cited consistently in sources from 2024-2026 with no narrowing trend. Key structural drivers:
- German supply overhang from lease returns and fleet disposals (Handlergarantie market)
- Italian new-car prices historically above EU average for premium brands
- Lower dealer density in South Italy = lower competition = wider retail margin achievable by dealer who imports vs buys local

**Assessment**: The price delta is **not compressing** in Q1 2026. Premium vehicle import remains structurally viable.

## 5. REGULATORY ENVIRONMENT

### 5.1 EU Cross-Border Vehicle Sales — No Changes in Q1 2026
- IVA mechanics unchanged: used vehicles (>6 months, >6,000 km) are taxed once in Italy at immatricolazione via F24 Elide
- New vehicles: IVA sempre a destinazione (Italy)
- Motorizzazione Civile telematically verifies IVA compliance before immatricolazione
- No new EU-level regulation affecting cross-border B2B vehicle sales confirmed in Q1 2026

### 5.2 Assonime Circolare 3/2026 — IVA on International Transport
- Issued in 2026, covers VAT on transport services for goods in export/transit/import
- Relevant to ARGOS's transport cost calculations but does not change vehicle import mechanics
- No operational impact on ARGOS's model

### 5.3 Emission Regulations — Watch Item
- EU EV mandate trajectory: Italy at 7% BEV market share in January 2026 (+2pp YoY)
- The diesel/petrol sourcing strategy of Italian dealers (import petrol, export electric) is likely to continue through 2026
- Long-term watch: EU 2035 ICE ban enforcement and its effect on premium used market valuation

1. **Exploit the success-fee asymmetry in every dealer touchpoint.** "Paghi zero finche' non hai il veicolo in concessionaria" — no competitor can say this. It is the single most powerful objection handler in the market.

2. **South Italy outreach remains a greenfield.** Confirmed: no competitor — B2C or B2B — has documented dealer outreach activity in Calabria, Basilicata, Puglia, Campania, or Sicily. ARGOS territory is uncontested.

4. **BMW/Mercedes/Audi are the right targets.** Confirmed by multiple independent sources as the top three premium used brands in Italy and the most traded cross-border. No strategic pivot needed.

5. **AUTO1 expansion in Italy is an opportunity, not a threat.** As AUTO1 buys FROM dealers to resell, an ARGOS-supplied dealer with premium inventory at good margin is exactly the profile AUTO1 would approach. Watch for coexistence.

6. **The first closed deal is the only missing asset.** All structural conditions are favorable. The market is growing (+10% Italy YTD February 2026), the delta is stable (15-30%), the territory is empty, the fee model is unique. Execution — specifically the first completed transaction — unlocks the review-driven trust that currently differentiates competitors.
